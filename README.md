## 📌 About StyleX

**StyleX** is a multi-salon and barber booking platform designed to make salon visits more convenient, organized, and time-efficient.

In a traditional salon experience, customers often have to visit the salon physically, wait in a queue, check whether their preferred barber is available, and ask about service prices and duration.

StyleX aims to change this experience by allowing customers to **discover salons, select services, choose their preferred barber, check available slots, book appointments, and track queue status** before visiting the salon.

The core idea is:

> **Choose your salon, choose your barber, choose your style, choose your time — and skip the unnecessary waiting.**

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Traditional salon and barber services face several challenges for both customers and salon owners.

## 👤 Problems Faced by Customers

### ⏳ Uncertain Waiting Time

Customers often visit a crowded salon without knowing how long they will have to wait.

### 💇 Preferred Barber May Be Unavailable

A customer may have a preferred barber or stylist, but there is usually no easy way to know whether that barber is available before visiting.

### 💰 Unclear Price and Duration

Customers may not always know:

- Service price
- Service duration
- Available services
- Barber availability

### 👥 Crowded Salons

Walk-in customers can create unnecessary crowds and long queues.

### 🔍 Difficult Comparison

Customers may find it difficult to compare:

- Different salons
- Barbers
- Services
- Prices
- Styles
- Availability

---

# 🏪 Problems Faced by Salons

Salon owners and barbers also face several operational challenges:

- Manual appointment management
- Difficult queue management
- Staff scheduling problems
- Difficulty managing service timings
- Unorganized booking flow
- Difficulty tracking appointments and revenue
